# Contributing

Contributions are welcome. Good first extensions include:

- more images and seeds,
- MRI k-space and CT-view splitting operators,
- DeepInverse `SplittingLoss` and `EquivariantSplittingLoss` baselines,
- multi-split validation averaging,
- additional no-reference stopping criteria.

Please keep experiments reproducible by recording command-line arguments and
writing summaries to `summary.json`.
